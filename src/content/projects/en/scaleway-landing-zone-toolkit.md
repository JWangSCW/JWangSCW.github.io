## What it is

Public, open-source repository delivering the Scaleway Landing Zone as code: network segmentation, IAM baselines, cost controls and observability primitives.

## Why it matters

Designed to be the standard starting point for partners and customers onboarding on Scaleway: built once, reused everywhere, maintained for the partner ecosystem.
